About Us

The Gateway of the Mediterranean

Strategically located in Oran, Algeria's vibrant port city.

Your Trusted Export Partner

EURL EL-AMIN EXPORT COMPANY is a young and dynamic enterprise based in Oran, Point du Jour, Algeria. We specialize in exporting 100% Algerian-manufactured products across the Mediterranean and beyond.

Our strategic location at the Port of Oran allows us to efficiently serve markets in Africa, Europe, and the Middle East. We are committed to showcasing the best of Algerian industry.

As a new company, we prioritize honesty, transparency, and building long-term relationships. We may be young, but our commitment to excellence is absolute.

What We Do

End-to-End Export Solutions

From sourcing to shipping, we handle every step of the export process.

Sea Freight

Full-container (FCL) shipping via the Port of Oran to Mediterranean, European, and global destinations. We handle shipments of at least one full container.

Customs Clearance

Complete documentation and customs handling for smooth export from Algeria. We navigate regulations so you don't have to.

Quality Control

Rigorous inspection at every stage. We verify product quality, packaging, and labeling before any shipment leaves our facility.

Packaging & Labeling

Export-compliant packaging with custom labeling in Arabic, French, and English to meet your market requirements.

Market Consulting

Leverage our expertise in target markets. We provide insights on product demand, pricing, and regulatory requirements.

Secure Payment

Trusted payment terms with documentation for every transaction. Letters of credit, T/T, and escrow arrangements available.
