About Us

The Gateway of the Mediterranean

Strategically located in Oran, Algeria's vibrant port city.

Your Trusted Export Partner

EURL EL-AMIN EXPORT COMPANY is a young and dynamic enterprise based in Oran, Point du Jour, Algeria. We specialize in exporting 100% Algerian-manufactured products across the Mediterranean and beyond.

Our strategic location at the Port of Oran allows us to efficiently serve markets in Africa, Europe, and the Middle East. We are committed to showcasing the best of Algerian industry.

As a new company, we prioritize honesty, transparency, and building long-term relationships. We may be young, but our commitment to excellence is absolute.
